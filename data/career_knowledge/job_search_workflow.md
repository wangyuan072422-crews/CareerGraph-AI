# Job Search Workflow

## Research a company

Start with the job description, the company's official site, and recent engineering material. Separate verified facts from assumptions. Record the source and date because company information changes.

For interview preparation, map each important requirement to one concrete example from your experience. Mark gaps honestly and prepare a learning plan for gaps that are important to the role.

## Track applications

Track the company, role, source, application date, current status, next action, and interview notes. A spreadsheet is enough at the beginning. Move the data into a database only when querying, filtering, or multiple users make it useful.

## Follow up professionally

After an interview, send a concise thank-you message that references a specific topic from the conversation. If a recruiter provides a timeline, wait until that timeline passes before asking for an update.
