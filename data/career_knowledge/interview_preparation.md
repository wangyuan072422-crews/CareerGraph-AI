# Interview Preparation

## Behavioral interviews

Use the STAR method: describe the Situation, Task, Action, and Result. Keep the answer focused on your own decisions and include a measurable result when possible. Prepare two or three flexible stories for teamwork, conflict, failure, ambiguity, and delivery.

Practice each story as a short spoken answer, then prepare likely follow-up questions. The goal is not to memorize a script. The goal is to recall the facts, tradeoffs, and outcome under pressure.

## Technical interviews

Build a preparation plan around the role's actual responsibilities. Review core concepts, practice explaining tradeoffs, and complete small exercises that resemble the work described in the job. For AI application roles, be ready to explain retrieval, tool calling, state management, evaluation, and failure handling.

Use mock interviews to practice thinking aloud. After each session, record one technical gap and one communication gap, then schedule a focused follow-up.

## Questions for the interviewer

Ask about the team's current priorities, how success is measured, what the first project would be, and how engineering decisions are reviewed. Questions should help you evaluate the role and show that you are thinking about the work.
